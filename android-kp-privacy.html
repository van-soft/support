KaleidoPen for Android

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KaleidoPen for Android (the “App”, “we”, or “us”) handles information when you use the App. KaleidoPen is a creative drawing, symmetry, animation, and media-export tool distributed through Google Play.

Privacy at a glance: KaleidoPen does not require an account, does not operate an account or content server, and does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. Your drawings, selected photos, presets, and settings are processed and stored on your device unless you choose to export or share them. Purchases are handled by Google Play.

1. Information Processed by the App

KaleidoPen processes the following information on your device to provide its features:

  • Creative content: strokes, colors, brushes, symmetry and path settings, imported photos, image-fill layers, canvas backgrounds, saved creations, and generated thumbnails.
  • Preferences: theme, language, guide and disc styles, sound, haptic, recording, export, brush, color, and symmetry settings.
  • Generated media: PNG images, GIF animations, and MP4 videos, including transparent-background images where supported.
  • Purchase status: product and purchase-state information returned by the Google Play Billing Library so the App can unlock purchased features.

This information is used only to operate the App. We do not receive your creative content or preferences.

2. Photos and Saving to Your Device

When you choose a photo for a fill or background, Android’s system photo picker lets you select the specific image you want to use. KaleidoPen receives and processes only the image you select. It is processed locally and may be stored as part of your artwork.

When you save an image, GIF, or video, the App adds it to the Pictures or Movies folder on your device. On Android 10 and later this needs no storage permission; on Android 9 the App asks for storage permission only to save the file. KaleidoPen does not scan or upload your photo library. The App does not request camera, microphone, location, or contacts permissions.

3. Audio and Recording

KaleidoPen can include the App’s own background music and sound effects in a recording. It does not record your voice or ambient audio. Video frames and audio are encoded on your device.

4. Local Storage and Deletion

Your creations and preferences remain in the App’s storage until you delete them, reset the relevant setting, clear the App’s data, uninstall the App, or reset the device. Android’s own device backup may include this data if you have enabled it; that backup is handled by Google under its policies. Media you save to your gallery, to files, or to another app is controlled by that destination and must be deleted there separately.

5. Purchases and Subscriptions

KaleidoPen offers monthly and annual auto-renewable subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase. Billing, payment credentials, refunds, subscription management, and purchase history are handled by Google through Google Play. We do not receive or store your card number or payment details.

The App receives limited purchase information from Google Play, such as the product identifier, purchase state, and purchase token, solely to unlock, acknowledge, restore, and manage access to purchased features. Google Play processes purchases under the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Information You Choose to Share

If you export or share a creation, the destination you choose—such as your gallery, a messaging app, social media, or another app—processes that media under its own privacy policy. KaleidoPen does not upload or publish your work.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, message, attachments, and other information you choose to provide. We use it to answer your request, provide support, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations, and retain it only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

7. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

KaleidoPen does not include analytics or advertising SDKs, does not use the advertising ID, does not create advertising profiles, and does not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell or share personal information.

8. Children’s Privacy

KaleidoPen is not directed at children under 13. The App can be used without creating an account or submitting personal information to us, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, contact us so we can review and delete it.

9. Security

We design KaleidoPen to minimize data collection and keep creative processing on your device. No method of storage is completely secure, so protect access to your device and keep backups of important exported work.

10. Your Choices

  • You can use drawing features without importing photos, saving to your gallery, or making a purchase.
  • You can remove creations in the App, clear the App’s data in Android Settings, or uninstall the App to delete everything it stores.
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions, and restore eligible purchases in the App.
  • You can ask us to delete support information you previously sent by email.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App, legal requirements, or our practices. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at qiweitech@gmail.com.
